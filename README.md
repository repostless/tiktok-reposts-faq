# TikTok reposts FAQ

Short, sourced answers to the questions people ask most about TikTok reposts: how to remove them, hide them, find them, and what other people can see.

Each answer links to a longer guide with steps and screenshots. Maintained by the team behind [Repostless](https://repostless.app/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=faq_repo). Corrections welcome: open an issue.

*Last checked against TikTok's Help Center: November 2026.*

## Removing reposts

### How do I remove one repost?
Open the reposted video, tap **Share**, then **Remove repost**. Pressing and holding on the video gets you to the same option.
→ [Delete a repost on TikTok, three ways](https://repostless.app/blog/delete-a-repost-on-tiktok?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=faq_repo)

### Can I delete all my reposts at once?
TikTok has no bulk delete. You can remove reposts one at a time, or use a tool that does those removals inside your own TikTok session. Never give your password to a tool that asks for it.
→ [Bulk delete all your TikTok reposts](https://repostless.app/blog/delete-all-tiktok-reposts-at-once?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=faq_repo)

### Is there a free way?
Removing reposts by hand is free and unlimited. Making your account private is free and instant, but it hides your reposts rather than removing them. Repost-removal apps usually have a capped free tier.
→ [Delete all reposts for free](https://repostless.app/blog/remove-tiktok-reposts-for-free?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=faq_repo)

### Can I undo a removed repost?
No. TikTok has no undo for a removed repost. You can repost the original again by hand while it still exists, which only works if you kept a record of what you removed.
→ [Can you undo a removed repost?](https://repostless.app/blog/export-and-restore-removed-reposts?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=faq_repo)

### Does removing a repost notify the creator?
TikTok does not document a notification for reposting or for removing a repost. That is not the same as "nobody is told". What is documented: while a repost is up, it carries your name and photo.
→ [Does removing a repost notify the creator?](https://repostless.app/blog/does-removing-a-repost-notify-the-creator?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=faq_repo)

### Can my account get banned for using a repost remover?
Nobody can promise it can't. TikTok's terms restrict automated access without written approval, and we found no public evidence of action taken over repost removal specifically. The risk mostly depends on how fast a tool works, how much it does in one go, and who holds your login. This is not legal advice.
→ [Can you get banned for using a repost remover?](https://repostless.app/blog/can-you-get-banned-using-a-repost-remover?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=faq_repo)

## Hiding and privacy

### Can I hide my reposts without deleting them?
TikTok has no setting that hides only the repost tab. To hide reposts from everyone except your followers, make your account private: **Profile → Menu → Settings and privacy → Privacy → Private account**. To hide them from one person, block that person. To hide them for good, remove them.
→ [How to hide reposts on TikTok](https://repostless.app/blog/how-to-hide-reposts-on-tiktok?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=faq_repo)

### Who can see my reposts?
On a public account, anyone who opens your profile can see your reposts, any text you added and the replies to them, and your reposts can show up in other people's For You feeds with your photo and nickname. On a private account, only your followers can.
→ [Who can see your TikTok reposts](https://repostless.app/blog/who-can-see-your-reposts-on-tiktok?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=faq_repo)

## Finding reposts

### Where is my repost tab?
Open your profile and tap the tab with the looping-arrows icon, next to your own videos.
→ [Find your repost tab](https://repostless.app/blog/see-your-reposts-on-tiktok?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=faq_repo)

### How many reposts do I have?
TikTok shows no repost total on your profile. Count by scrolling the repost tab, or let an app scan it.
→ [Check your repost count](https://repostless.app/blog/check-tiktok-repost-count?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=faq_repo)

### My reposts aren't showing on my profile
Usually you're on the Videos grid, have no reposts yet, or are looking at a private account you don't follow. Still missing? Update the app, clear its cache and sign back in.
→ [Reposts not showing: causes and fixes](https://repostless.app/blog/tiktok-reposts-not-showing-on-profile?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=faq_repo)

### The Repost button is missing
TikTok documents no cause for this. Update the app, open the video from your For You feed, check your account for restrictions, and clear the cache.
→ [Repost button missing](https://repostless.app/blog/tiktok-repost-button-missing?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=faq_repo)
